SchemaOutputBenchmark for 3.0.1: the named streams of Field providers in a portable seeded Schema.

This is not a full dashboard run: only SchemaOutputBenchmark ran, three times, one after another, on
one otherwise idle machine, with the publication protocol of METHODOLOGY.md. Each operation generates
or writes 250 records of twelve Field references, seeded with 7.

Environment: Temurin 21.0.13+9, Darwin 27.2.0 arm64, Apple M5 Pro, JMH 1.37
Command: ./gradlew :benchmarks:jmh -PjmhArgs="SchemaOutputBenchmark -foe true -wi 3 -i 5 -f 3 -t 1 -prof gc"
